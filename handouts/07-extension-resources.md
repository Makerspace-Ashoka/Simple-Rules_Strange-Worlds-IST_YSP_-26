PIXELS THAT THINK: EXTENSION RESOURCES

Continue exploring cellular automata with these resources:

ONLINE TOOLS & SIMULATORS:

• p5.js Web Editor
  https://editor.p5js.org/
  Create and share p5.js sketches online

  https://devinacker.github.io/celldemo/

• ConwayLife.com

• "A New Kind of Science" by Stephen Wolfram (online)
